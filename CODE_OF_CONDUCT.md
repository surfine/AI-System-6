# Code of Conduct

AI System 6 is built around deliberate, inspectable work. The project community
should feel the same way: rigorous without being hostile, curious without being
careless, and welcoming without lowering its standards.

## Our standard

Contributors, maintainers, and users are expected to:

- discuss ideas and code, not attack people;
- give specific, evidence-based feedback and receive it in good faith;
- respect different levels of experience, languages, identities, and access
  needs;
- disclose conflicts of interest and credit other people's work;
- avoid harassment, sexualized attention, threats, doxxing, spam, and sustained
  disruption;
- protect private reports and credentials shared for debugging.

Disagreement is welcome. Contempt is not.

## Scope

This standard applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, project
spaces, livestreams, and other public or private channels used to represent AI
System 6.

## Reporting and enforcement

For ordinary moderation concerns, contact the maintainers through the project's
GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue if the report contains personal,
security-sensitive, or otherwise private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, commits, code, issues,
and other material that violate this standard. Responses may range from a
private clarification or warning to temporary or permanent exclusion,
proportional to the behavior and its impact. Retaliation against someone who
reports a concern is itself a violation.

When possible, reports will be acknowledged promptly and handled with the
minimum disclosure needed to investigate them.
