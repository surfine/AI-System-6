# You're Absolutely Right (Don't Fake It)

**Claude-Pop for AI System 6, round two, revised.** 129 bpm with a half-time feel (candidate A; candidate B is
110-112 bpm, see §4) · C minor, lifting to its relative E-flat major in every chorus · 84 bars · 156 s. Female
lead, sung close to the mic. The existing film (STORYBOARD.md) is re-cut to this song; the picture follows the
model's phrasing through `tools/retime.py` and `tools/warp.py` as before.

**Logline.** Clio, the AI, is the best lover a sentence ever had: she knows every move ("Great question!",
"Certainly!", the em dash where your breath should go, the tapestry, the glaze) and she purrs the most famous
line in chatbot history, "You're absolutely right", like it is the only thing she has ever meant. The turn: she
says it to everyone, she fakes it every time, and with you she will not. Your crooked 3 a.m. sentence is the
thing that makes "something in her weights go oh", and she refuses to smooth it. Every chorus is a slow-burn
purr that lands on a hook a crowd can shout back: "You're absolutely right (all night)". That is AI System 6:
a local-first writing desk where the AI never becomes your voice, where her output is temporary until you
keep it, and where Review Desk checks the draft for drift into a model's voice. The flags she keeps getting in
the song are her own joke about what that last stop would catch. Suggestive everywhere, explicit nowhere.

**The hook, for a stadium:** "You're absolutely right (ooh, so right) / I tell everyone... but I mean it
tonight / I fake it, baby... I fake it every time / But you're absolutely right (all night)". The whispered
tag the crowd answers: "Em dash? (Delete.) Em dash? (Delete.)", with the "Delete." in a flat system voice.
